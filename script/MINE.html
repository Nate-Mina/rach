ACT ONE — THE ARCHIVE

FADE IN:

EXT. ROCHESTER, NY — NIGHT (PRESENT)

Snow falls on a tired industrial city. A single lit window in a row of apartments.

INT. NATE'S APARTMENT — NIGHT (PRESENT)

NATE MINA, early 20s, wiry and restless, sits at a glowing laptop. The browser tab reads: "rachelandnate.com — The Truth about Rachael and Nate."

On the desk: a digital camera, a memory chip, a half-full whiskey glass. A voice recorder blinks red, still warm from a long recording. Stacks of printed transcripts — the voice clips — are paper-clipped to the monitor.

NATE (V.O.)
Everything you're about to see is true. I didn't make any of it up. I just wrote it down so I'd know what actually happened — between her and me. And I kept the recordings.

He hits ENTER. The page goes live. On the screen, a girl mid-laugh at a birthday party.

NATE (V.O.)
This is the story of a girl, a husband she didn't choose, and the six months I spent believing she'd pick me. And then the part I never put on the site: how it all blew up.

THE NIGHT WE MET

EXT. CITY NIGHTCLUB, ROCHESTER — NEW YEAR'S EVE 2010 — NIGHT

Neon. Bass you feel in your ribs. A wall of bodies counting down. NATE (20) works the room, a cruise-ship network engineer between contracts.

A mutual friend, MARY (20s), leans in over the noise.

MARY
That girl — Rachael. She thinks you're hot. Go dance with her.

Nate finds her: RACHAEL SLACK (20), dark-eyed, magnetic even in a crowd, celebrating her birthday. He offers a hand. She takes it.

ACTION BEAT — they dance close, careless, two strangers on the last night of the year. Nate pulls out a point-and-shoot, snaps a photo of her laughing, and asks to send it to her. That's how he gets her number.

THE LONG DISTANCE

EXT. CRUISE SHIP DOCK — FLORIDA — DAWN (JAN 1, 2011)

Nate, duffel slung, boards a crew shuttle. His phone lights up — a text from Rachael.

INSERT — PHONE SCREEN

RACHAEL: "still thinking about last night. text me?"

NATE (V.O.)
I left the next morning for six weeks, doing network engineering on the cruise ships in the Bahamas. We texted every day. Called when we could.
MONTAGE — THE COURTSHIP AT SEA

- Nate on a ship deck at 2 a.m., wind in his hair, phone to his ear, grinning.

- Rachael sends a selfie: sleepy, happy. "wish u were here."

- Nate circles FEB 14 in red on a crew-cabin calendar.

- A long email from Rachael: "I can't wait to see you. Save Valentine's Day for me?"

NATE (V.O.)
By week three I was counting down. I asked her out for Valentine's Day. She said she couldn't wait.

THE PROMISE OF FEBRUARY

EXT. GREATER ROCHESTER INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT — NIGHT (FEB 13)

Nate lands, snow crunching, breath fogging. Home.

INT. NATE'S APARTMENT — MORNING (FEB 14, VALENTINE'S DAY)

Nate arranges flowers, buzzing. He calls her.

INTERCUT — RACHAEL'S HOUSE

Rachael, pajamas, cord twisted around her finger.

RACHAEL
Nate... there's something. I've been kinda talking to this other guy. John. He's in Army bootcamp, in Virginia.

Nate's smile dips.

RACHAEL (CONT'D)
It doesn't matter. We're just talking. Not like us. I still want to see you.
NATE
(on phone)
...Okay.
NATE (V.O.)
She said it didn't mean anything. I believed her. That's the first mistake.

INT. NATE'S APARTMENT — BEDROOM — THE NEXT TWO DAYS

They go on the date — amazing. The next day, amazing again. The third day, they get fully intimate, "also fucking amazing." After that, inseparable.

NATE (V.O.)
Me and her were like inseparable. Meanwhile, she was still talking to John — but she talked to him on the phone right in front of me. Told me everything. Because she was hiding nothing from me, I trusted her more.

INT. NATE'S APARTMENT — DAY

Rachael on the phone with John, in the same room, Nate listening. John's voice, small through the speaker:

JOHN (V.O.)
I miss my home. I just want to kill myself. This sucks so much.
NATE (V.O.)
From their conversations, I heard it — he was failing push-up tests, depressed, doing badly in bootcamp. And if she broke up with him, he probably would've offed himself. So I agreed she could wait. Wait till he got home.

ACT TWO — THE SECRET

THE TRIP / THE MARRIAGE

INT. NATE'S APARTMENT — NIGHT (LATER)

Nate paces, phone to ear, jaw tight.

NATE
(on phone)
You're going to his bootcamp graduation? Rach — he's gonna marry you.
INTERCUT — RACHAEL

Rachael, almost offended.

RACHAEL
Nate, do you think I would just get married like that? I love you. If I ever married anyone, my whole family would be there.
NATE
(on phone)
So you promise? Pick me when you get back?
RACHAEL
I promise. Have fun while I'm gone.
NATE (V.O.)
She told me to have fun. Meet girls. I had zero interest in anyone but her.
MONTAGE — THE WEEK SHE'S GONE

- Rachael calls less. John's voice bleeds through before she hangs up fast.

- A call: John, raw: "GO DIE, NATE!" — then dead. Later, John calls Nate directly, stiff apology. Nate realizes Rachael forced him to say sorry.

- Nate broods with LONNA (level-headed) and MARY, who eyes him hopefully.

EXT. ROCHESTER CLUB LINE — NIGHT

Nate waits in line. Phone rings — Rachael, crying.

RACHAEL (PRE-LAP)
You replaced me. With Mary. I don't mean anything to you?
NATE
(on phone)
What? Rach — I'm thinking about you right now.

INT. RACHAEL'S HOUSE — MORNING (WHEN SHE RETURNS)

Nate helps with her bag. Notices a luggage tag — a different last name.

NATE
Did you get married?
RACHAEL
Well... legally, yes.
NATE
What the fuck? You said you were gonna break up with him!
RACHAEL
I know. I made a mistake. I'm sorry.
NATE
We'll get an annulment.
RACHAEL
I will. I just need time.
NATE (V.O.)
Months go by. I told her she had to pick. I even tried to break up with her. I told her to leave.

THE OTHER GIRL / THE RECOMMITMENT

INT. NATE'S APARTMENT — NIGHT

Nate, hurt, starts seeing MARY — funnily enough, the same girl who'd recommended Rachael to him. Drinks. Talking.

EXT. NATE'S STREET — NIGHT

Rachael drives by, sees Mary's car, texts Nate: "Who's at your house?"

INTERCUT — NATE
NATE
(texting)
What does it matter? You got married.
RACHAEL
(texting)
Please, please just get rid of her. I wanna see you right now. Please.

Nate, still in love, kicks Mary out — "super asshole of me" — and Rachael comes over. They make up. Makeup sex, "phenomenal."

NATE (V.O.)
So I gave her a week to choose. After the week: "I need more time." Another week. That went on for about three weeks. I didn't even know what my emotions were doing.

THE THREE CHOICES

INT. NATE'S APARTMENT — NIGHT (THE FIGHT)

They fight. Rachael, cornered:

RACHAEL
My parents want me to date him. They're both Catholic. I'm just gonna tell him...
NATE
I'll give you three choices. Pick him — and tell him you've been seeing me the whole time you've been married, and before. Or pick me — and tell him it's just not working and you're sorry. Or... I tell him everything. Because it's not fair to anybody.
RACHAEL
If you tell him that, I'll just say you're a creep and you made all this up in your head.

Nate goes quiet. A challenge. Internally, something turns.

NATE (V.O.)
That was a challenge to me. So a day later, I got drunk. And when I was drunk, I decided I was gonna send him everything.

ACT THREE — THE LONG GOODBYE (THE RECORDINGS)

NATE (V.O.)
Before it ended, there were the recordings. The voice clips I kept. This is how it actually sounded — July of 2011, the two of us, pretending a normal life was still possible.

THE PREGNANCY SCARE — JULY 7

INT. NATE'S APARTMENT — NIGHT (JULY 7, 2011)

Nate on the phone, laptop open, half-listening, half-capturing. Rachael's voice, frayed at the edges.

RACHAEL
I'm trying to make an appointment for my migraines and they're giving me a hard time... they're like, "you're too old for this," and it's just kind of painful.
NATE
So... you might be going to Germany as early as next week?
RACHAEL
No. I said on my Facebook if I were to go it could be as early as next week. That's not going to happen.
NATE
How do I know?
RACHAEL
I'll be like five minutes, okay? I got to go over to Julie and Louie's and I'm taking my sister.
NATE
You want to hang out after that?
RACHAEL
Tomorrow afternoon. And I'll sleep over.
NATE
All right. You can call me like 20 minutes after. It's just when you don't text me for the whole entire day. If you just said "hey, I'm busy" — instead of making me worry, thinking maybe something's wrong with the blood tests.
RACHAEL
I haven't even talked to the doctor about my blood tests yet.
NATE
When you said you were on the phone with the doctor all morning, I thought you might have an STD. Or... maybe you're pregnant.
RACHAEL
That's right. Shit, that's very well possible. And if I am, it's yours.

Beat. Nate sits up.

NATE
Why would it be mine?
RACHAEL
Because.
NATE
What would you do? Would you keep it?
RACHAEL
I'm not ready for a kid.
NATE
I always was like, "okay about abortion" — but I think if it was my own, I'd think twice. 'Cause it's just like killing...
RACHAEL
That would mess up my life so badly right now.
NATE
It would change it, for sure.
RACHAEL
We'll talk about it tomorrow. I just want to lie on the couch with you all night.
NATE
You want me to go get the second season of Heroes?
RACHAEL
I don't really care. I just want to lie on the couch with you.

Nate softens.

NATE
It's okay, baby.
RACHAEL
My mom's upset because she's like, "you lost your GPS!" They're yelling at me for that.
NATE
Why didn't you say it got stolen?
RACHAEL
I did!
NATE
So how is that your fault? Did you tell them you've been using one?
RACHAEL
No.
NATE
Everything's my fault, you said.
RACHAEL
I'm always getting blamed. I should be more responsible, I guess.
NATE
You are. I love you.
RACHAEL
It's fine.
NATE
I was just freaking out 'cause I thought something was really wrong.
RACHAEL
That's really all that's wrong. I'm just going crazy. Me and Becca are going over to Julie and Louie's. I didn't get much sleep — I didn't go to bed till 5.
NATE
You said you woke up at noon. I've called you so many times today.
RACHAEL
I know.
NATE
So why'd you ignore it?
RACHAEL
I didn't ignore it, I'm an idiot. You could just pick it up and say, "hey, can I call you back?"
NATE
Tomorrow afternoon, then.
RACHAEL
After two.
NATE
After two means like four or five.
RACHAEL
I'm seeing you tomorrow, that's all I know, okay? I really got to go. Becca's waiting. Bye.
NATE
Bye.

Nate sets the recorder down. Stares at it.

NATE (V.O.)
A pregnancy scare. A stolen GPS she swore she'd reported. Every sentence a small negotiation with the truth.

THE BOYFRIEND QUESTION — JULY 12

INT. NATE'S APARTMENT — NIGHT (JULY 12, 2011)

Two phones, two voices, the easy banter of people who've forgotten they're supposed to be ending.

NATE
Why are you so nosy about Buffalo? "Why are you going to Buffalo?"
RACHAEL
Huh?
NATE
Life's all about having fun, give me give me now — that's how little kids are.
RACHAEL
I agree.
NATE
So... I bought — what's her name on my Facebook? On my Friends for Sale.
RACHAEL
You bought my name?
NATE
I bought you.
RACHAEL
Hi. You already owned me.
NATE
(laughs)
I bought the other you, too.
RACHAEL
What other me?
NATE
So what are you up to?
RACHAEL
Just checking my email. Then I'm going to bed.
NATE
I miss you.
RACHAEL
I miss you, too.
NATE
You're adorable. I'm glad I got to at least see you today. It was only like half an hour, very short, but it was nice.
RACHAEL
(groans)
My phone is dying.
NATE
Just plug it in. We used to talk on Skype — your Skype is shitty, or what?
RACHAEL
I'm on Skype right now. On somebody else's computer.
NATE
Oh. You know a girl by the name of Rachael Slocum?
RACHAEL
Do I know what?
NATE
It said you were a mutual friend. Weird. I thought I saw... Anyway. So what are you doing?
RACHAEL
Nothing. Just finding out everything. My sister's being a brat, going through my closet. Someone's in my room! Hey, can I call you back in ten minutes?
NATE
Yeah. All right, night.
RACHAEL
Good night.

THE VOICEMAILS — JULY 13

INT. NATE'S APARTMENT — NIGHT (JULY 13, 2011)

Nate plays back her voicemails, transcribing them by hand. We HEAR them, overlapping his quiet room.

RACHAEL (V.O. — MESSAGE 1)

Hey, it's me. I'm just calling to say hi and see how your day's going. I was super lost, couldn't find a car shop... I wanted to call and say hi and I hope I get to see you. Miss...

RACHAEL (V.O. — MESSAGE 2)

Hey, it's me. I was calling to see how your night went. I'm going over to Jenny's... I get how it is. I'm on my way over to Jenny's, so I guess I'm not coming over to say good night. Give me a call — your mom was asking me about some conference thing, Thursday night? Kisses, bye.

RACHAEL (V.O. — MESSAGE 3)

You just call and say good night? That's just... bye.

Nate stops the playback. The room is very quiet.

NATE (V.O.)
Three messages in one night. None of them the truth she was living. But the wanting was real.

THE TRUTH WEAPON

INT. NATE'S APARTMENT — NIGHT (DRUNK, SOON AFTER)

Nate at his laptop. Types. Builds a site: rachelandnate.com. Journals. Voice clips. Pictures of him and her "all over the world." He stops.

NATE (V.O.)
Not any rated-X pictures. I wasn't about to do that. Just the truth. But it was too much for one email, so I made a website instead. And I hadn't sent it anywhere.

INT. NATE'S APARTMENT — NIGHT (SHE FINDS OUT)

Rachael: "I'm just gonna tell him that." Nate, calm:

NATE
Just go to rachelandnate.com.

Two seconds later — tires screech. Rachael bursts in, running for his computer.

RACHAEL
I'll break it so it's not there anymore!
NATE
It's not on my computer. Even if you killed me, it'd still be up.

She softens, sucking up: break up with him, I'll take it down. She doesn't.

THE THREE-WAY

INT. NATE'S APARTMENT — NIGHT

Rachael three-ways John, stage-whispers: "tell him we haven't been talking." Nate:

NATE
You know what, fuck that. Hey John — I'm sorry. We've been seeing each other the whole time, and I didn't feel it was fair to lie to you anymore. Go to rachelandnate.com.
NATE (V.O.)
Boom. That was that. I thought he'd break up with her. But she was so apologetic... promised him never again. And then she moved to Germany with him.

DETECTIVE BALABAN

INT. NATE'S APARTMENT — NIGHT (A YEAR LATER)

A year on. Nate drinks a lot. Throws big parties — a hundred people, underage drinking, he doesn't know half of them. A normal-dressed man about his age approaches.

MAN
Let's go over here and talk. I'm Detective Balaban.
NATE (V.O.)
Oh shit. There's a whole bunch of shit going on here. This probably isn't legal.

INT. NATE'S APARTMENT — QUIET CORNER

BALABAN
Do you know a girl named Rachel? The one you made a website about. Apparently it got thousands and thousands of hits.
NATE
(heavy)
I'd forgotten the password. I didn't even know it was up.
BALABAN
People were harassing her parents. Calling her a slut. My boss asked if you'd take it down.
NATE
Just asking? Well, maybe. But if I'm not being told to... I guess I won't. Plus I don't have the password.
NATE (V.O.)
So it completely blew up in her face. I didn't intend for anybody but him to see it. I completely destroyed her life because of that. But I didn't say anything that wasn't true.

EPILOGUE — A YEAR LATER, THE CALL

INT. NATE'S APARTMENT — NIGHT (EVEN LATER)

Phone rings. Rachael's name. Nate answers, guarded.

RACHAEL (V.O.)
I want to thank you. I was really lying to everybody. After that, I kind of changed my views on life.

Nate sits with it. The whiskey goes still.

NATE (V.O.)
So yeah. Was I the asshole? I don't know.

CLOSE

INT. NATE'S APARTMENT — NIGHT (PRESENT)

Back to Nate at the laptop. The cursor blinks at the end of the last entry. Outside, Rochester snow falls on a city that never knew any of this happened.

NATE (V.O.)
She told me once, "I love you." Asked me to let her go — said she didn't want that. So I'm still here. Still waiting for her to choose.

He closes the laptop.

FADE OUT.
THE END

NOTE TO PRODUCTION:

Adapted from the real personal archive "The Truth about Rachael and Nate" (2010-2011), Nate's recorded first-person account ("Rachael Story"), and the July 2011 voice recordings between Nate and Rachael (voice/7-7-11, 7-12-11, 7-13-11vmail). Names, dates, and the central love-triangle are drawn directly from that record; the act-three dialogue is transcribed verbatim from the recordings and dramatized as performed scenes. The recording reveals material not on the site: John's Army bootcamp depression and Nate's decision to let Rachael wait rather than risk his suicide; the three-choice ultimatum; the origin of rachelandnate.com as Nate's drunken truth-weapon; Detective Balaban's visit after the site went viral; and Rachael's later call thanking Nate.
