ACT ONE — THE ARCHIVE

FADE IN:

EXT. ROCHESTER, NY — NIGHT (PRESENT)

Snow falls on a tired industrial city. A single lit window in a row of apartments.

INT. NATE'S APARTMENT — NIGHT (PRESENT)

NATE MINA (22), wiry, restless, a cruise-ship photographer by trade, sits at a glowing laptop. The browser tab reads: "The Truth about Rachael and Nate."

On the desk: a digital camera, a memory chip, a spent cup of coffee. A half-filled bottle of whiskey.

NATE (V.O.)
Everything you're about to see is true. I didn't make any of it up. I just wrote it down so I'd know what actually happened — between her and me.

He hits ENTER. The page goes live.

NATE (V.O.)
This is the story of a girl, a husband she didn't choose, and the six months I spent believing she'd pick me.

A beat. He opens a folder of photos. The first: a girl mid-laugh at a birthday party.

THE NIGHT WE MET

EXT. CITY NIGHTCLUB, ROCHESTER — NEW YEAR'S EVE 2010 — NIGHT

Neon. Bass you feel in your ribs. Confetti cannons. A wall of bodies counting down.

A mutual friend, MARY (20s), leans into Nate over the noise.

MARY
That girl — Rachael. She thinks you're hot. Go dance with her.

Nate scans the crowd. Finds her: RACHAEL SLACK (20), dark-eyed, magnetic even in a crowded room. She's celebrating her birthday.

He crosses. Offers a hand. She takes it. They fall into the music.

ACTION BEAT — they dance, close, careless, two strangers on the last night of the year. Nate pulls out a point-and-shoot and snaps a photo of her laughing.

CUT TO:

INT. RACHAEL'S FRIEND'S HOUSE — AFTER-PARTY — LATER

Rachael, very drunk, has passed out on a couch, shoes off, smile still on her face. Nate stands over her with a friend, uncertain.

NATE (V.O.)
I drove her to a friend's after-party. She passed out the second we got there. I took her number and let her sleep.

He types her number into his phone: RACHAEL — BIRTHDAY GIRL.

THE LONG DISTANCE

EXT. CRUISE SHIP DOCK — FLORIDA — DAWN (JAN 1, 2011)

Nate, duffel slung, boards a crew shuttle. His phone lights up — a text from Rachael.

INSERT — PHONE SCREEN

RACHAEL: "still thinking about last night. text me?"

NATE (V.O.)
I left the next morning for six weeks in the Bahamas, working the cruise ships. We texted every day. Called when we could.
MONTAGE — THE COURTSHIP AT SEA

- Nate on a ship deck at 2 a.m., wind in his hair, phone pressed to his ear, grinning.

- Rachael sends a selfie: sleepy, happy. Caption: "wish u were here."

- Nate counts days on a crew-cabin wall calendar. He circles FEB 14 in red.

- A long email from Rachael: "I can't wait to see you. Save Valentine's Day for me?"

NATE (V.O.)
By week three I was counting down. I asked her out for Valentine's Day. She said she couldn't wait.

THE PROMISE OF FEBRUARY

EXT. GREATER ROCHESTER INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT — NIGHT (FEB 13)

Nate lands, snow crunching under his boots, breath fogging. He's home.

INT. NATE'S APARTMENT — MORNING (FEB 14, VALENTINE'S DAY)

Nate in the kitchen, awkwardly arranging flowers, buzzing with nervous energy. He calls her.

NATE
(into phone)
Happy Valentine's Day. I got you flowers. You ready?
INTERCUT — RACHAEL'S HOUSE

Rachael, still in pajamas, twirling the phone cord.

RACHAEL
(on phone)
Nate... there's something. I've been kinda talking to this other guy. John.

Nate's smile dips.

RACHAEL (CONT'D)
It doesn't matter. We're just talking. Not like us. I still want to see you.
NATE
(on phone)
...Okay.
NATE (V.O.)
She said it didn't mean anything. I believed her. That's the first mistake.

ACT TWO — THE SECRET

INT. NATE'S APARTMENT — DAY (FEB 14, CONTINUED)

Nate picks Rachael up. She introduces MARIA (20s), her ride-or-die friend, all sharp humor.

They go to dinner — small, sweet, a little awkward. Back at Nate's, they fool around with zip ties, get tangled, topple onto the bed laughing.

NATE (V.O.)
That's where the first kiss happened. Stuck together on my bed, laughing so hard we forgot to untie ourselves.

INT. NATE'S APARTMENT — BEDROOM — NEXT DAY

Quieter. Tender. They sleep together for the first time.

INT. JULIE & LOUIS'S LIVING ROOM — FEB 16

Rachael's older friends JULIE and LOUIS host. Rachael and Nate cuddle on the couch watching LAW & ORDER: SVU — her favorite. She rests her head on his shoulder, completely at ease.

NATE (V.O.)
By Wednesday she'd introduced me to the people who mattered to her. I thought that meant something.

EXT. DUNKIN' DONUTS — DRIVE-THRU — DAY

They order coffee, goofy, in love. Nate snaps a photo of her mid-sip.

NATE (V.O.)
The next day I had to leave again. Six more weeks. Bahamas.
CUT TO:

THE ENGAGEMENT

INT. SHIP CREW BERTH — NIGHT (MARCH)

Nate on the phone, the satellite connection crackling. His face falls.

NATE
(on phone)
Wait — engaged? Rachael, what do you mean engaged?
INTERCUT — RACHAEL'S KITCHEN

Rachael, twisting the cord, eyes wet.

RACHAEL
I think... I think I made a mistake. I don't know what I was doing. I like you. I like you so much.
NATE
(on phone)
Then break it off. You can break it off.
RACHAEL
I will. I'll figure it out. I promise.
NATE (V.O.)
She promised she'd end it. I went back to work loving her harder, because I thought I was the one she'd chosen.
MONTAGE — SPRING APART

- Nate, night after night, walking to the ship's phone bank, feeding coins, talking an hour past when he can afford it.

- Rachael sends "I miss you" texts at 3 a.m.

- He tells his sister: "She's it. She's the one."

THE REUNION / NIAGARA

EXT. EASTVIEW MALL PARKING LOT — DAY (APRIL)

Nate and Rachael collide in an embrace. His dad waits nearby, polite, giving them space.

INT. RESTAURANT — NIGHT

Nate, Rachael, and Nate's DAD share a booth. Normal. Happy. A family-in-progress.

EXT. SCUBA SITE — DAY

They dive together — underwater shots, weightless, her hand in his.

NATE (V.O.)
For ten days it was perfect. Then I shipped out again. Spain. France.

INT. HOTEL ROOM, NIAGARA FALLS — MAY 5

Nate's friends on a guys' trip. A strip club earlier; now a hotel hot tub, drinks flowing. Rachael, tipsy and glowing, pulls Nate close. The room blurs into laughter and bad decisions.

FRIEND (O.S.)
Holy shit, it's a live porno in here.

Nate and Rachael crack up. For one night, the world is just them.

NATE (V.O.)
I won't forget that weekend. None of us will.

THE TRIP / THE MARRIAGE

INT. NATE'S APARTMENT — NIGHT

Nate paces, phone to his ear, jaw tight.

NATE
(on phone)
You're going to his Army graduation? Rach — he's gonna marry you. He's already got you engaged, he leaves for Germany next week. This is exactly what he'll do.
INTERCUT — RACHAEL

Rachael, defensive, almost offended.

RACHAEL
Nate, do you think I would just get married like that? I love you. If I ever married anyone, my whole family would be there.
NATE
(on phone)
So you promise? It won't change anything with us?
RACHAEL
I promise.
NATE (V.O.)
She told me to go have fun. Meet girls. Party. I had zero interest in anyone but her.
MONTAGE — THE WEEK SHE'S GONE

- Rachael calls less and less. John's voice bleeds through the line before she hangs up fast.

- A call: John's voice, raw: "GO DIE, NATE!" — then the line dies.

- Later, John calls Nate directly, stiff apology. Nate realizes Rachael forced him to say sorry.

- Nate broods with LONNA (his level-headed friend) and Mary, who eyes him a little too hopefully.

EXT. ROCHESTER CLUB LINE — NIGHT

Nate waits in line with friends. His phone rings — Rachael, crying.

RACHAEL (PRE-LAP)
You replaced me. With Mary. I don't mean anything to you, do I?
NATE
(on phone)
What? Rach — I'm thinking about you right now. The whole time.

INT. RACHAEL'S HOUSE — MORNING (AFTER SHE RETURNS)

Nate sits across from Rachael. She's jumpy, circling something.

NATE
Did you get married?
RACHAEL
What do you mean by that?
NATE
It's a simple question! Did you get married?

A long beat. She crumbles.

RACHAEL
(soft, crying)
Legally... I got married.

They both break. Nate's eyes flood; she covers her face.

EXT. MOUNT HOPE CEMETERY — DAY

Gray skies. They walk among old headstones, two ruined kids.

RACHAEL
My family's Catholic. They hate divorce. I might be stuck with him.

She kisses him, gentle, and wipes his cheek.

RACHAEL (CONT'D)
Don't cry.
NATE (V.O.)
She'd promised she wouldn't. And I could see on her face it was a mistake. So I stayed. And we kept seeing each other. And she kept lying to him.

ACT THREE — THE WEB TIGHTENS

HOUSE-SITTING / THE FIGHT

INT. JUDY'S HOUSE (RACHAEL HOUSE-SITS) — NIGHT (MAY 27-29)

Nate and Rachael, alone in someone else's home, playing house. He sleeps there every night — while on the phone, she tells John she's alone.

EXT. MARIA'S BACKYARD — PARTY — SATURDAY, MAY 28

S'mores by a fire. Maria, ELISE, and Elise's boyfriend JAMES. Rachael slips away for Slurpees, comes back smiling, and sends Nate a bikini photo under the table.

Nate pulls James aside by the fire.

NATE
I don't even know if she's been leading me on this whole time.
JAMES
(shakes his head)
Man. It's all messed up.

INT. JUDY'S HOUSE — BEDROOM — SUNDAY MORNING

Rain hammers the windows. Nate's phone BUZZES — a picture message. John, half-naked. It also hit his email.

NATE
(realizing)
He replied-all to the bikini pic she sent us both.

Nate, annoyed, sends John a shirtless selfie back. John calls Rachael instantly.

INTERCUT
JOHN (O.S.)
Is Nate there?
RACHAEL
(into phone, forced)
No, babe, he's not —

Nate's voice cuts in from off-screen. John hears it. Silence. Then rage.

INT. RACHAEL'S CAR — DRIVING — MOMENTS LATER

John's incoming email notification. Nate reads it over her shoulder: "STAY AWAY FROM MY WIFE. STOP STALKING HER."

RACHAEL
We have to go. He wants to talk to you.

She grabs the phone. Nate tries to keep it.

NATE
I'm sick of the lies — let me talk to him!
RACHAEL
GET OUT OF MY CAR!

She rips the battery out. Yanks the memory chip. Flings it on the floor.

RACHAEL (CONT'D)
There's evidence on here he could use to divorce me!
NATE
Everything's also on my computer. Give me the chip or I tell him everything.

She produces it from her pocket. They walk the street, she lies smoothly into the phone to John. Nate's dad arrives, gets the motorcycle started. Nate leaves.

THE CONFRONTATION

INT. NATE'S APARTMENT — THAT EVENING

Nate opens his laptop, starts a Skype call to John. Before it connects —

The door BURSTS open. Rachael, ferocious, slams the laptop shut.

RACHAEL
You contacted him?! I'll destroy this — all of it!

She digs her nails into his arm, wrestles him to the floor. He struggles, stunned.

RACHAEL (CONT'D)
(low, lethal)
Don't you ever contact him again. Or I call the cops and tell them you raped me.

She grabs a cup of water, goes for the laptop — he blocks her — she throws the water across him instead.

NATE
(panting)
I won't. I won't.

She leaves. He sits on the wet floor, shaking.

NATE (V.O.)
That's the night I broke a little. And the night I went looking for the truth.

THE LIST

INT. NATE'S APARTMENT — NIGHT (DRUNK)

Nate, whiskey in hand, remembers her Facebook password. Logs in. Scrolls. His face goes still.

INSERT — FACEBOOK NOTE, TITLED "GUYS"

A list. Fifteen names. One simply: "That RIT guy" — she didn't even know his name. Further down: BRAD JOHNSTON — her married boss, the one she swore nothing happened with.

NATE (V.O.)
Fifteen. She talked to all of them still. The boss she'd lied about. I didn't know who she was anymore.
MONTAGE — UNRAVELING

- Nate drinks, parties with Lonna, tries to numb it.

- He sees a girl — the same friend who first pointed him to Rachael — for a few days. It changes nothing.

- Rachael calls: "Who's at your house?" Shows up twenty minutes later, grabs him, hugs him like she's drowning. "How could you see someone else?"

NATE (V.O.)
She was furious I'd moved on. From the girl who was married to someone else.

He sends her away. She comes back the next day. They fall into each other again.

THE COVERS

INT. NATE'S APARTMENT — JUNE 13

Rachael lounges, cuddling, when John calls. She mouths a plan: use a fake friend, "Miranda."

Nate runs next door, briefs neighbor DANA (19). Dana plays along on the phone — "Yeah, me and Rachael went to beauty school, three years." John buys it. Barely.

INT. WEGMANS, PENFIELD — JUNE 14

To prove she's "with Phoebe," Rachael meets Phoebe, snaps a photo, sends it to John. Then slips out, jumps on Nate's motorcycle, rides to his place. They watch HELLRAISER, cuddled, kissing.

NATE (V.O.)
Every alibi needed a photo. Phoebe. Lonna. Miranda. A whole cast of invented friends to keep one husband calm.

INT. EMS STORE, PITTSFORD — JUNE 16

Rachael, still on the phone with John, gets grilled about Brad — the boss — and about me. She lies smoothly, then shoots Nate a look: "my hands were cold, it was nasty" — the opposite of the truth.

They buy climbing gear "for Rochester." Find Lonna. Another photo for John: "See? With a girlfriend, not Nate."

THE ROAD TRIP

EXT. HIGHWAY TO NYC — NIGHT (JUNE 18)

Rachael, Nate, and Lonna in a car, laughing, free for the first time in weeks. Neon of the city rises ahead.

FLASHBACK CUT — EARLIER THAT DAY

Rachael called Nate, crying, shaking: she "can't go." He knows why — John. Nate drives to her house, drags her out to have fun anyway.

INT. NYC HOTEL / STREETS — NIGHT

They explore the city, giddy, unobserved. For a few hours there's no husband, no lies. Just three kids in New York.

NATE (V.O.)
On the way John called. He was furious she'd gone. He said she "wasn't supposed to." That's when I knew — it was never love he had for her. It was ownership.

THE DECISION THAT NEVER COMES

INT. NATE'S APARTMENT — LATE JUNE / EARLY JULY

Nate at his laptop (PRESENT bleeds in). The "archive" builds: photos, the John email, the journal.

NATE (V.O.)
June 26, she said she'd choose. John or me. Germany or here. She gave herself till July 3.

INT. RACHAEL'S HOUSE — JULY 3

She calls. Can't decide. Loves them both. Needs more time.

NATE (V.O.)
July 3 came. Then went. No decision. My friends said I was crazy. Tell him everything, they said. But I wanted it to be her choice.
MONTAGE — THE LONG GOODBYE

- Skrillex concert, Water Street Music Hall: $80 tickets, them screaming the drop, alive.

- Rachael, July 7, on the phone: "I might be pregnant. If I am... it's yours."

- Nate, recording her voice, saving every word like evidence of a love he's losing.

INT. NATE'S APARTMENT — NIGHT (PRESENT)

Back to Nate at the laptop. The cursor blinks at the end of the last entry.

NATE (V.O.)
I gave her a week to choose. It's been two. She says, "Keep being awesome and I'll pick you." I don't know what to believe.

He looks at the screen. The site is live. The whole truth, out in the world.

NATE (V.O.) (CONT'D)
She told me once, "I love you." Asked me to let her go — said she didn't want that. So I'm still here. Still waiting for her to choose.

He closes the laptop. Outside, Rochester snow falls on a city that never knew any of this happened.

FADE OUT.
THE END
