About This Site

This website is a digital archive and intimate chronicle of the relationship between Rachael and Nate. It is a collection of memories, conversations, photographs, videos, and journal entries gathered during the time we spent together.

Unlike a typical website, this space is deeply personal — a place to revisit moments that mattered, to reflect on what happened, and to preserve pieces of a story that deserves to be remembered.

What You'll Find

  • Photos — visual memories, including trips to NYC and everyday moments.
  • Conversations — recorded audio conversations and discussions.
  • Videos — video recordings capturing moments in time.
  • Journal — personal reflections and written thoughts from Nate.
  • Audio — phone recordings and other audio files.
  • Communications — emails and written exchanges.

Why It Matters

In an increasingly digital world, having a dedicated space to preserve memories becomes more and more important. This website stands as a testament to the value of documentation, the importance of being present, and the power of revisiting what once meant something to us.
